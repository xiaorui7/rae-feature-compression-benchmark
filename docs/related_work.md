# Attribution and research boundary

The official [RAE implementation](https://github.com/bytetriper/RAE) and the paper [Diffusion Transformers with Representation Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11690) supply the pretrained encoder, Stage-1 decoder, latent-statistics convention, and motivation. This project does not claim authorship of those weights or reproduce full Stage-2 training. The existing undergraduate thesis studied spatial RAE latent PCA/projection and a compact denoising proxy; those artifacts remain separate and are not silently pooled into the V1 main table.

[DINOv2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07193) motivates frozen visual representations and linear/kNN evaluation. The V1 spatial-mean RAE feature protocol is **not** DINOv2's official ImageNet probe protocol. [PCA](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.2015.0202) is a conventional linear reconstruction baseline. The V1 biased linear adapter is a lightweight engineering comparison, not a novel compression theorem; squared-error linear autoencoding has a strong relationship to PCA. The simple logistic probe is trained *after* the compressor is fixed, avoiding a task-driven two-linear-layer rank argument as primary evidence.

The brief also mentions [Matryoshka Representation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13147) and [Matryoshka-Adaptor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20243) as possible future inspiration. Neither method is implemented or claimed as reproduced here. CIFAR-10 class Precision@10 is only a coarse labeled-neighbor proxy, not commercial visual search relevance. No V1 result is evidence of improved DiT training, FID/KID/IS, or generated image quality.
